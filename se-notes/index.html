Software Engineering: Notes

Built from the SE unit slides (R23CP2409). Each topic has a definition, an explanation (characteristics and sub-components), and extras such as diagrams, comparisons, pros and cons, and memory tips.

Unit 1 · Intro & Process Models

SE definitions, the eras of software, characteristics, applications, the generic view, myths, SDLC, Waterfall, Incremental, RAD, Prototype, Spiral, Concurrent, CBD, PSP, TSP, Agile, XP.

Unit 2 · Requirement Engineering

Requirement types, functional vs non-functional, the RE process and its 7 tasks, negotiation, analysis modelling, data modelling, cardinality and modality, OOA, scenario, flow and class-based modelling, SRS.

Unit 3 · Design Engineering

Design process and objectives, abstraction, modularity, information hiding, refinement, functional independence, the design model, architecture, cohesion, coupling, architectural styles.

How each topic is laid out

The highlighted box is the definition. Learn it as written for exam answers.
Green boxes are memory tips and mnemonics.

not in slides means something was added because it's commonly asked in exams but isn't on the slides.

The slides' diagrams (Spiral, RAD, cohesion spectrum and others) were images, so they have been redrawn here as simple text diagrams.
